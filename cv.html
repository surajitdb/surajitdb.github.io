Career

  1. Jan 2025 – present Assistant Professor (Selection Grade), SMVITM Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Shri Madhwa Vadiraja Institute of Technology and Management, Bantakal, Udupi. Before this appointment, adjunct faculty in the same department (Nov–Dec 2024), teaching object-oriented programming with Java.
  2. Nov 2023 – Oct 2024 Senior Assistant Professor, Alva's Institute of Engineering and Technology Mangaluru, Karnataka. Taught artificial intelligence and machine learning.
  3. Apr 2023 – Jan 2025 Climatematch Academy (volunteer, remote) Impact Scholars mentor (Oct 2023 – Jul 2024), project teaching assistant for seven student groups working on Earth's albedo and land-cover change and on regional precipitation variability and extremes (Jul 2023, Certificate of Excellence), and job board manager.
  4. Jul 2015 – Jul 2020 Doctoral researcher and teaching assistant, NITK Surathkal Department of Water Resources and Ocean Engineering, funded by an MHRD institute assistantship. Ran undergraduate laboratory classes, including strength of materials for civil engineering.
  5. Sep 2011 – Sep 2013 Pre-doctoral researcher, University of Auckland Auckland, New Zealand. Trained in bivariate copulas for hydrological modelling, and in LC-MS and HPLC laboratory methods.
  6. Jan 2009 – Jul 2011 Assistant Professor and Lecturer, Central Institute of Technology, Kokrajhar Assam. Taught food engineering courses covering heat and mass transfer and fluid mechanics.
  7. 2008 Teaching assistant, IIT Guwahati and NIT Silchar Engineering graphics for first-year civil engineering at IIT Guwahati (Jul–Dec 2008), and laboratory classes at NIT Silchar (Jan–May 2008).
  8. Nov 2004 – Jul 2006 Research assistant and guest lecturer, NERIST Department of Agricultural Engineering, North Eastern Regional Institute of Science and Technology, Itanagar. Research assistant on the MHRD-funded project "Landslide hazard zonation of Dikrong River basin using remote sensing and GIS", junior project fellow on a water-harvesting tank study, and guest lecturer.
