Flood risk

Where floods are likely, and how far to trust the map.

PASCHIM-FSM

Manuscript in preparation
Problem
Flood susceptibility maps for India's west coast rarely say how confident they are, which limits their use in planning.
Approach
A Google Earth Engine pipeline with Random Forest, comparing four uncertainty methods: Bernoulli variance, margin confidence, bootstrap Random Forest variance and inductive conformal prediction.
Result
AUC 0.92 on Dakshina Kannada, with runs extended to other coastal districts including Udupi.
  • Google Earth Engine
  • Random Forest
  • Conformal prediction
  • Remote sensing

Cross-region transferability of flood maps

In progress
Problem
A susceptibility model trained in one district is often applied in another without checking whether it still works there.
Approach
Train and test across two contrasting districts, coastal Udupi and semi-arid Raichur, and measure whether the uncertainty intervals keep their stated coverage after the move.
  • Spatial transferability
  • Coverage analysis
  • Google Earth Engine

Monsoon streamflow intervals

In progress
Problem
Monsoon streamflow shifts regime abruptly at onset and withdrawal, which breaks the assumptions behind standard prediction intervals.
Approach
Apply change-point-aware conformal prediction (CPTC; Sun and Yu, NeurIPS 2025) to daily streamflow of the Netravathi River at Bantwal.
  • Conformal prediction
  • Change-point detection
  • Streamflow

xLSTM for long-horizon groundwater forecasting

Proposal
Problem
Daily groundwater level forecasts degrade quickly over long horizons, and their error bars are rarely checked.
Approach
Compare xLSTM with LSTM, RNN and N-HiTS on more than 20 years of daily records from three wells in different countries, with conformal prediction intervals.
  • xLSTM
  • N-HiTS
  • PyTorch
  • Conformal prediction
