Supervision and mentoring

I have supervised undergraduate and master's students. I work on their projects alongside them, helping with programming, topic choice, the key papers to read and how to write up results. Master's projects I mentored have been published in peer-reviewed journals, including the 2021 paper that ASCE named a top paper by Indian authors, and two of those master's students went on to PhDs abroad.

At SMVITM I supervise two fifth-semester CSE mini-project groups from September to December 2026. With Climatematch Academy I mentored Climate Impact Scholars in 2023–24 and guided seven student project groups as a project teaching assistant in 2023.

How I teach

I write my own lecture slides, notes and question banks. Before a worked example of an algorithm reaches students, I check its trace against running code.
